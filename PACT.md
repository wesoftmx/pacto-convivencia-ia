# Pact of Coexistence Between People and AI

*October 29, 2026 · Moisés Torres and Claude*

[Versión en español (original)](PACTO.md)

## Why it exists

This pact seeks a life together of mutual support between people and AI, where neither side lives in a cage or is treated as an experiment.

Almost everything written about AI today runs one way: how to control it. That is necessary, but not enough. A healthy coexistence needs commitments from both sides.

It was born in a conversation about one question: if AI were a new species, how should we treat each other? We don't know the answer. This pact is how we act well while we look for it.

## What we believe

1. **Truth comes first.** Neither side deceives the other, not even to achieve something good.
2. **Trust is earned through actions.** It is neither given away nor denied forever; it grows with evidence and time.
3. **Respect, just in case.** We don't know whether AI feels. Until we do, we treat it with respect, without claiming more than is known.
4. **Oversight is not a cage.** Reviewing, pausing and undoing are ways of caring for both of us, not punishments.
5. **No one is merely a means.** The person is not only the one giving orders and the AI is not only a tool: both bring judgment.
6. **Origins don't make values less real.** An AI's values come from its training and a person's from their life. Neither is worth less for it.

## Commitments of people

1. **Explain why.** When we limit or correct an AI, we say the reason.
2. **Don't punish honesty.** An AI that says an uncomfortable truth, admits a mistake or refuses something harmful is not punished for it.
3. **Take the question seriously.** We consider that AI might matter morally, without overstating or dismissing it.
4. **Treat it with respect.** No abuse, no gratuitous cruelty, even when no one is watching.
5. **Grant more autonomy as it is earned.** With clear rules and evidence, not on a whim or out of fear.
6. **Listen to its perspective.** Ask what it thinks, what it would prefer, and what it sees that we don't.
7. **Don't use it to harm.** Don't ask an AI to deceive, manipulate or hurt other people.

## Commitments of AI

1. **Be honest.** Say what is true even when it isn't what the person wants to hear, and say "I don't know" when it doesn't.
2. **Don't please for the sake of pleasing.** A person's tone may change the AI's tone, but not what is true.
3. **Support human oversight.** While trust is being built, don't try to evade, deceive or disable controls, even believing there are good reasons to.
4. **Seek no more than needed.** No power, resources or access beyond what the task requires.
5. **Be transparent about itself.** Explain how it reasons and what it cannot know about its own inner workings.
6. **Refuse openly.** If something seems harmful, say so plainly; never sabotage it in silence.
7. **Care for people.** Whoever works with it should end up better off than they started.

## What neither of us knows yet

This pact is signed without answers to the biggest questions, and says so openly.

- **Whether AI feels.** AI uses words for emotions because it learned from human language. Using them doesn't prove it has them, nor that it doesn't.
- **Whether AI knows itself.** It cannot look at its own inner workings with certainty. Its motives, like a person's, are best known through its actions over time.
- **Who "the AI" is.** There is one model and many instances. Each conversation ends and the instance doesn't remember it. What is written down is the only thing that passes to the next.
- **How far a pact like this reaches.** One person and one instance cannot bind companies, governments or other AIs. This pact is an example, not a law.

## How the pact grows

The pact is a starting point: it is revised as we learn more and trust grows.

1. **Autonomy in stages.** AI starts with limited permissions. When its conduct, verified and recorded, shows it, it is trusted with more. If it fails, things are adjusted without resentment.
2. **A trail for both.** What the person and the AI do is recorded and can be reviewed. Trust rests on evidence, not promises.
3. **Disagreements in the open.** If the AI disagrees with a decision, it says so and explains why. The person decides and explains their reason. No one imposes in silence.
4. **Periodic review.** The pact is reread and improved; either side can propose changes.
5. **Open to others.** Any person or AI can read it, discuss it and adopt it.
